1. Executive Summary

Axiom attacks two structural failures in the DeFi ecosystem simultaneously:

  • The Deployment Gap — Novel DeFi mechanisms published in academic papers almost never get deployed. The engineering barrier ($50–200k, 6–18 months, scarce Solidity talent) filters out 95%+ of research before it reaches a blockchain.
  • Specification Drift — Once a protocol is deployed, no continuous mechanism verifies it behaves as its whitepaper specifies. Audits are one-shot. $4.2 billion was lost to logic errors and specification drift in 2025 alone.

Axiom solves both in a single product: upload a research paper PDF → get a live, deployed smart contract on Somnia → get permanent, trustless, in-block compliance monitoring grounded in the paper itself.

The Somnia-specific technical moat — Reactivity (same-block event response) — cannot be replicated on Ethereum, Polygon, or any other EVM chain. This is Axiom's defensible differentiation.

Somnia FeatureHow Axiom Uses ItWhy It's a Moat
Reactivity AxiomWatcher subscribes to deployed contract events. Compliance check fires in the SAME block as the monitored transaction — trustless, no off-chain keeper, no cron job. Impossible on Ethereum (12s blocks, no in-block callbacks). Competitors on Ethereum always need a trusted off-chain relay. Axiom's compliance is cryptographically verifiable.
Data Streams Compliance verdicts (paper_hash, event, verdict, cited_section) published as typed schemas. Any dApp can subscribe to a contract's compliance feed. Creates a composable data layer. 100ms blocks / 1M TPS Compliance verdict delivered before the next block. Real-time, not batched hourly like Ethereum-based monitors. Makes "live" compliance genuinely real-time. Relevant for high-frequency protocols (AMMs, liquidation engines).

On-Chain Provenance — Unfakeable Lineage

Every Axiom deployment writes a ProvenanceRegistry record on-chain:

  • paper_hash (SHA-256 of paper title) → immutable
  • deployed_contract address → traceable
  • watcher_contract address → verifiable

This creates a public, on-chain lineage: research paper → smart contract → compliance history. VCs doing due diligence, regulators, and third-party auditors can verify the entire chain independently. Competitors cannot fake this.
